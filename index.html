Real AI generation

Generate images & video

Connect easy@Ai to Hugging Face Inference Providers. Your token stays in this page's memory and is sent to the local bridge only when you generate. No paid fallback is configured.

Free-use reality: Hugging Face currently lists $0.10/month in Inference Provider credits for free users, subject to change. Image/video requests may use these credits quickly; video may be unavailable on the free allowance. The app does not buy credits or switch to paid billing automatically. Check current pricing ↗

1 · Connect your free Hugging Face token

Create a fine-grained token with “Make calls to Inference Providers” permission. Paste it below. It is not written to localStorage and is cleared when you reload the page.

Checking local AI bridge is available when this page is served by start_windows.bat or start_mac_linux.sh.

2 · Text-to-image

Generate a real image through Hugging Face Inference Providers. The chosen model must currently have an available provider.

When a reference image is supplied, easy@Ai uses Qwen Image Edit to transform that image according to your prompt. This can help retain visual details, but is not a guarantee of identical faces.
Your generated image will appear below.

3 · Image-to-video

Animate a generated image or upload a starting image. Provider support and free access vary; video generation may fail if the model is not served or credits are unavailable.

Video generation is slower and may not be included in free credits.

Important privacy and cost controls

  • Use a fine-grained token with inference permission only; never share it or include it in screenshots.
  • The token is held in page memory, not saved in the app's local project data. Reloading clears it.
  • This hosted app is reachable by anyone who has its URL. Your Hugging Face token is sent only with your requests and is not saved by this app. Do not share your token or the app URL publicly if you want privacy.
  • Hugging Face free credits are limited. easy@Ai makes no paid fallback requests; if free quota is exhausted, it shows an error.
  • Character details are inserted into image prompts. Text-to-image alone does not guarantee identity preservation; video reference conditioning depends on the model.
